Safety Architecture

Human oversight by design

Autopilot runs autonomously within defined trust boundaries. Every action with real-world consequence — contacting a real person, touching a production system, capturing real payment — stops and waits for explicit human approval before executing.

"Thirty Labs runs autonomously until it reaches a trust boundary:
outreach, secrets, production databases, or real payment capture."
